# FUTURE_DS_03 – Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses a bank marketing campaign dataset to understand customer subscription rates and identify customer groups with higher conversion potential.

## Dataset
Bank Marketing Dataset – UCI Machine Learning Repository.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel Online
- Data Analysis
- Charts and Data Visualization

## Key Insights
1. Overall subscription rate is approximately 11.52%.
2. Single customers have a higher subscription rate (13.96%) than married customers (9.90%).
3. Tertiary-educated customers have a subscription rate of 14.30%.
4. Customers with a successful previous campaign outcome have a high subscription rate of 64.34%.
5. Customers contacted through cellular and telephone methods have higher subscription rates than customers with unknown contact methods.

## Recommendations
1. Focus marketing campaigns on customer groups with higher subscription rates.
2. Reuse effective strategies from successful previous campaigns.
3. Improve customer contact data quality.
4. Test different messages and offers for groups with lower subscription rates.
5. Monitor subscription rates regularly to improve campaign performance.
